A QUICK REFRESHER

The house rules.

A simple game. A lifetime to master.

01

Know your colours.

The room creator takes reds and breaks; the second player takes yellows. Pot one of your colours legally to keep your turn.

02

Line it up. Let it fly.

Click the cloth to aim at a point, or drag the wooden cue to rotate it around the white. Pull the power handle down and release to shoot. A longer pull means a harder shot.

03

Add a little finesse.

Move the dot on the cue ball: top for follow, bottom for draw, left or right for side spin off the cushions. Arrow keys on the table fine-tune aim; Space shoots.

04

Play a clean shot.

Hit your own colour first. Missing every ball, hitting an opponent’s ball or the black first, or potting the white is a foul. After contact, a ball must reach a cushion or a pocket. A foul cancels all your remaining visits and gives your opponent two visits. Legal pots keep the current visit going; a legal shot without your own pot uses one visit. The white stays where it stops unless you pot it: only then may your opponent click open cloth to place it anywhere.

08

Save the best for last.

Clear all seven of your colours before taking a shot at the black. Pot it legally to win. Pot it early, or foul while potting it, and you lose. Colours stay down after fouls. A rematch swaps colours and the break.
